* **Windows:** 10/11 64-bit
* **Processor:** 2 GHz
* **RAM:** 2 GB
* **Graphics Card:** Onboard, at least 1280 x 768 resolution
* **Sound Card:** Onboard
* **Available Disk Space:** Minimum of 1.5 GB for installation
